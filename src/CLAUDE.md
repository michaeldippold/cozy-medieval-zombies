# `src/`: the engine's rules and the code map

Read with the root `CLAUDE.md`. This file says what is, not when; the rulings behind each line are in `docs/02` by date.

## Rules the code keeps

- Tiles are 64×32. Art is drawn at whole pixels in world space; never scale in `drawImage`. Never draw UI into the canvas.
- The world fills the window at one of three zoom levels (`view.js` `ZOOM_LEVELS`), applied by the GPU; the interface has its own scale, capped at 2x. Nothing is laid out against 960×540. DOM over a world point goes through `view.toUi`.
- Nothing materialises in view. The active region's boundary stays well beyond the viewport.
- The map is stored block by block (`map.js`); every tile access goes through `map.tileAt`. A missing chunk is grown on demand: plain floor inside the authored bounds, wild forest with a few of the dead beyond them. **Reading never grows a chunk:** growth is the simulation's ring round each player (`map.ensureAround`, four chunks) and the mutators' (`growTile`); a read beyond the ring sees the frozen blank tile; the renderer, the ground, the light, and sight never change the world, and the ground bakes a chunk only once its neighbours are grown (`check("growth")`).
- **The map is generated, never typed:** `python -I tools/mapgen/valley.py` writes `data/maps/valley.json` from a description per place. A floor definition's `walk` (false, or a speed factor) and `drink`, and a wall definition's `wall.seeThrough`, are how water, shallow water, fences, bars, and arrow slits work; nothing in code names them. A see-through wall bounds no room. A building's `wall` may be per side, `partitions` make rooms inside it, a `fence` feature walls a rect with gates; an opening may be authored `open`, `broken`, or boarded (`boards: { in, out }`); a placeholder box definition carries a `label`, drawn on the box.
- **A change to anything on a chunk says so:** what a guest must see marks the chunk (`map.touch`, `markDirty`, or a mutator that calls them); what only the save keeps (a fuel between units, a timer, a stamp, health short of a break, a bag made on first use) calls `map.unsaved`. The save keeps each chunk's form until one of them drops it (`game.serialize`); `game.verifySaveCache` catches a miss (the server's log says THE SAVE'S CACHE MISSED); `node tools/savecache.js` proves the list.
- Rooms are found by flood fill, never authored. Walls are state on tile edges (`n` and `w` slots, Zomboid style), never objects; a door or a window is a wall with a kind and a state.
- The ground is one baked picture per chunk (`ground.js`, `docs/15`), drawn as one quad; nothing draws the floor per tile and nothing on the ground plane alternates by tile parity. A floor changes only through `map.setFloor`.
- Light is one function (`light.js`). Nothing else computes light. Light feeds sight. In the renderer every vertex carries the world point its light is read from; nothing is lit in screen space.
- The sway: the poke's stamp and the renderer's lean read the same clock, `loop.simTime()`; a wall-clock read on either side switches every wobble off. A sprite definition turns the sway off with `"sway": false` or replaces it with a `clickAnim` (the well).
- Drawn art comes in through sprite definitions (`data/sprites/*.json`, read by `assets.js`; `docs/17`): packed `images` with top-vertex and per-frame anchors, `drawings` with mirrors for facings, two-tile furniture sliced per tile along a vertical screen line; a crop's rows follow the furrows of its soil.
- The sim rolls only through `rng.js` (seeded from the map) and reads the clock only through `loop.simTime()`; a new `Math.random` or `performance.now` in a sim file is a bug. `game.js` reads no window and no DOM: the whole world runs headless under `tools/world.js` and hashes the same.
- Per-player state lives on the player, never in a module-level variable; the world holds a list of players and the dead hunt the nearest they see.

## The world and its state

- `game.js`: the world's state, `create` from a map file, the step order (`begin`, `step`), `serialize` and `restore` (the save and the join's format), `hashText`, `syncParts` (what `check("sync")` compares), `verifySaveCache`.
- `map.js`: tiles, edge walls, collision, rooms, authoring (sets, features, scatter, wild), `propById`, `feedFire`, growth and eviction.
- `world.js`: the world's time scale (1 awake, `sleepTimeScale` from `data/tuning.json` while every living player sleeps; `game.timeScaleNow`) and the id counter every prop and entry takes its instance id from.
- `visibility.js` line of sight · `sight.js` how much of the dead in view a player sees in the dark (the client's own, from its own light; `docs/07`) · `light.js` the one light function, its per-chunk cache, portals · `clock.js` the day · `rules.js` the sim's rules (a snapshot of the settings for a browser's world, the save's for a server world; carried in the save since version 2) · `tuning.js` the entities' numbers from `data/tuning.json` · `rng.js` the one seeded generator · `events.js` the event bus · `iso.js` the projection · `pathfind.js`.
- `verbs.js`: the verb table; `verbs.run(name, world, player, target, args)` with targets as addresses; rows are defined where they belong (`sleep` in `entities/player.js`, `craft` in `stations.js`, `dip` in `burn.js`).
- `stations.js`: what a prop is as a station, the recipes (`data/recipes.json`), ingredients reached across the bag, the belt, and every container within reach, the queue stepped in the world's time, the output bag beside the station, the `craft` verb (`docs/30` section 6).
- `fruit.js`: fruit trees (a sprite definition's `pick` block): the `pick` verb, a bare tree bearing again on its clock day (`fruit.step`, in `game.begin`; `regrowAt` rides on the prop, so the save and the chunk to a guest carry it), #190.
- `interactions.js`: anything on anything from `data/interactions.json`, "Nothing interesting happens" by default; a row may name an `action` verb. `use.js`: the Use mode (an item picked, the next click its target).
- `placement.js`: the ghost, carried props, building, removal; `performPlacement` is the one path for host and guest (`docs/13`).
- `interact.js`: doors and shutters on `E`, the right-click actions, the clickable things that wiggle, click animations, `keyEvents` and `beltEvents` (E, B, T, the belt keys 1 to 4 as events in a player's snapshot, performed by the world's step), `CLICK_REACH`.
- `items.js` the registry, `makeEntry` (every entry stamped with a `uid`), loot rolls, `hitsPerKill`, `wearPerHit` · `inventory.js` and `grid.js` bags and the tetris grid · `moodles.js` hunger, thirst, tiredness, bleeding, infection · `bleed.js`, `infection.js` wounds and the infection marker · `burn.js` burning as a status of the dead (`ignite` is every source's one call: a weapon's `ignite` chance, the fireball and a fire arrow always; the `dip` verb makes fire arrows at a lit fire) · `fire.js` a burning body drawn.
- `combat.js` the swing, the shove, damage, the targets' outline, `wearWeapon`, `tryAttack` with its `predict` mode for a guest (`docs/14`, `21`) · `archery.js` the bow's and the spear's draw, projectiles in flight, the landing marker (`docs/20`) · `buttons.js` the mouse buttons as roles (`docs/21`).
- `entities/`: `character.js` the shared character model (the player, the dead, and villagers wear items and share sheets by outfit, say lines; a dead one is a container whose body is redrawn as it is stripped; `docs/19`), `player.js` (movement and aim from a snapshot, never the keyboard), `zombie.js`, `villager.js` (idle, wander on a tether, answer a poke), `movement.js` the walking the dead and the villagers share.

## Rendering and the figure

- `gl.js` the WebGL2 batch, the atlas, the area textures · `render.js` a hub over `render/` (`scene.js` the frame, the night pool, fog, occlusion holes; `area.js` the light textures; `walls.js` drawn walls, caps redrawn on the sky, posts, boards, leaves; `props.js` props and their animations, the ghost; `entities.js`) · `ground.js` the ground baked per chunk · `assets.js` the sprite loader (drawn sheets, furniture slicing, facings; a sheet baked on first sight) · `sprites.js` facings and frame names · `render-util.js` shading · `placeholders.js` the dev art generators · `camera.js` · `view.js` the three zooms and `toUi`.
- `figure.js` a hub over `figure/`: `rig.js` the body's numbers (`BODY`) and the poses, `raster.js`, `draw.js` the garments and the sheets, `held.js`, `wardrobe.js` (`docs/12`, `32`; a body number is tried from the console before it is ruled, `docs/testing.md`).

## Multiplayer

- `net.js` the wire (`docs/31-multiplayer/04` status, `09`): the join a load, then frames every third step at normal speed (`FRAME_EVERY`); a player's record in light and heavy parts, the dead, the villagers, and the corpses as deltas with a roster when membership changes; a guest's own player runs the controller in predict mode and the hit comes from the host; a guest's inputs as runs while the world runs fast; `PROTOCOL` (4), the browser id in the join, the owner key (`setOwnerKey`), the host secret (`setHostSecret`), `setSyncReports` and `setTestSnapshot` for `check("sync")`, `status().wire` for a frame's bytes. The default wire is the Fly relay (`RELAY_URL`, `wss://cmz-relay.fly.dev`); `?relay=none` is the loopback of one browser's tabs, `?relay=ws://localhost:8012` a local relay.
- `host.js` the authority's half, shared by the browser host and the server world: joins and whose character is whose (a join over the cap, `playerCapBrowser` or `playerCapServer` in `data/tuning.json`, is refused with `full` before anything is made or taken from the save), dormancy (a guest away sixty seconds of game time goes dormant into the save and comes back on the rejoin), stepping away (a player idle five minutes in a shared world), the world's events answered for the players they are for, the routing of a message or a log line to the acting guest; `MAX_OWNERS`.
- `save.js` the worlds store (IndexedDB `cmz`/`worlds`, the index in localStorage; `VERSION` 2 with `save.upgrade` the migrations; an autosave every 60 s of game time) · `identities.js` identities (a name and a look), the browser id, the friends' worlds bookmarks, the owned keys · `loop.js` the fixed step, hit-stop, `advance`, the sim clock, the pause count, and `setBackgroundDriver` (a Worker's tick steps a world with friends in it while the tab is hidden; a solo world stays stopped).

## The client

- `main.js` the client, which only reads the world: boot's stages, the worlds flow (`__game.worlds`), the view, the keys, the panels, `render()` and `renderUi()`. `boot.js` the loading shell and the crash screen. `input.js` keys and the mouse. `player-controller.js` what the mouse and keys do to the player, from an input snapshot. `settings.js` localStorage. `dev.js` `window.__game`. `build.js` the build id the deploy stamps.
- `ui/`: `hud.js` the portrait, health, stamina, the hands line, the clock · `prompt.js` the prompt and messages · `zombie-bars.js` · `speech.js` bubbles · `swing-timer.js` the swing bars and the exposure bar at the bottom centre · `moodles.js` · `context-menu.js` · `inventory-panel.js` the bag and the character screen, two independent panels, with `inventory-actions.js` the handlers and the slot rules (on a guest the same handlers as commands by uid, slot, and bag address) · `station-panel.js` the one panel for every station (Make calls the verb and nothing else) · `build-palette.js` the build palette on `N` · `screens.js` title, the Worlds screen, identities, character creation, pause with Open to friends, settings, controls, the owner's screen, dead · `kit.js` the `ui_kit` sheet as CSS custom properties (`style.css` puts the stone on the bag, the character screen, and the belt alone) · `cursor.js` the three hands as a CSS cursor, a settings choice · `log.js` the chat log, bottom-left, the chat box at its foot · `names.js` names over heads.
